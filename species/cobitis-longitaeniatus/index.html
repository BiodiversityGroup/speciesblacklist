Cobitis longitaeniatus

Actinopterygii

The Yleng spined loach (Cobitis ylengensis), Ngô's spined loach (Cobitis squataeniatus), longitaeniatus spined loach (Cobitis longitaeniatus) and Phong Nha spined loach (Cobitis phongnhaensis) are all known only from limestone mountain streams within the Phong Nha-Ke Bang drainage.
