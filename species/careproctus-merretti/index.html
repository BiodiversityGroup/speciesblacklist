Careproctus merretti

Merret's snailfish

The finely-speckled snailfish (Careproctus aciculipunctatus) and Merret's snailfish (Careproctus merretti) are each known only from a single specimen collected from the Porcupine Basin off the southwestern coast of Ireland.
